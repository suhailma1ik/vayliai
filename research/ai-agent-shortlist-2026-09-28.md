# Solo-founder AI agent shortlist

Research date: 28 September 2026. Assumes US$10,000 monthly recurring revenue, international sales, no existing buyer network. These are opportunities to validate, not proven gaps or revenue forecasts. Competitor product pages establish available features and advertised prices, not independently verified customer outcomes.

## Recommendation

Test a tender-readiness agent for one class of UK public-sector supplier first (e.g. commercial cleaning companies that bid regularly). Second choice: RFQ-to-quote drafts for one industrial distributor category with explicit SKU identifiers. Start with paid, manually reviewed pilots. Productivity reduces build time; distribution, customer trust, data access and exception handling remain limiting factors.

|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | Tender-readiness and submission checklist for one supplier niche | $500 | 20 | 2–4 weeks |

All prices are hypotheses for volume-capped offers. Estimates assume experienced development, sample data, narrow uploads/exports, and human approval; production integrations and procurement take additional time. Setup fees are not MRR. Gross revenue is not profit.

## 1. Tender readiness

Input: tender pack, amendments and approved supplier evidence. Output: cited mandatory requirements, deadlines, missing evidence, draft responses to supported questions and a submission checklist. Keep final qualification and submission with the buyer. Start with uploads, not a global tender crawler.

Stotles advertises Bid Studio Growth from £990/month, with 15 bid/no-bid reports and 5 bid projects. Tenderlake UK+IE Monitor is £325 monthly or £249/month paid annually; Analyst is separately metered. These demonstrate active competition, not an empty market. The proposed angle is a configured workflow for one trade and repeat bidders, not merely a cheaper document summarizer.

Sources: [Stotles pricing](https://www.stotles.com/pricing), [Tenderlake pricing](https://www.tenderlake.com/pricing/index), [Stotles qualification workflow](https://help.stotles.com/how-to-qualify-bids-harder-and-faster-with-stotles-ai).

Distribution hypothesis: find relevant suppliers through public contract award notices, then demonstrate a requirements checklist on a public tender. [Official Find a Tender guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-short-guides/buyers-and-suppliers-how-to-use-the-central-digital-platform-the-enhanced-find-a-tender-service-html) confirms award notices contain awarded supplier details. [Contracts Finder](https://www.gov.uk/contracts-finder) also supports historical contract research. Validate bidding frequency and willingness to pay before assuming subscription retention.

## 2. Distributor RFQ drafts

Input: emailed PDF/Excel requests, authorized catalog, price list and customer pricing rules. Extract and match exact SKUs, flag unknowns, calculate via deterministic rules and prepare a quote for approval. No invented compatibility, prices, availability or substitutions. Initially export CSV/PDF instead of writing to an ERP.

[Conexiom](https://conexiom.com/) already automates RFQs and orders. [Sorsivo](https://sorsivo.com/) markets RFQ-to-quote software for industrial distributors; [QuickQuote](https://quick-quote.in/) addresses distributor sales with catalog matching and pricing. The segment-specific fit and onboarding advantage need proof; no reliable directly comparable numeric public price was established in this review.

Distribution hypothesis: manufacturer authorized-distributor lists, distributor websites and narrowly targeted inside-sales managers. Replay 20 historic RFQs with permission; compare draft creation and correction time against the existing workflow. Catalog cleanup and pricing complexity are the principal build risks.

## 3–5. Supporting opportunities

See [supporting research](ai-agent-opportunities-supporting.md) for invoice exceptions, bookkeeping and agency delivery workflows. For staffing invoice exceptions, the narrow proposed product assembles the approved timesheet, purchase order and invoice evidence when paperwork blocks payment. Chaser already offers AI reply classification/drafting, so generic reminders are insufficient differentiation. [Chaser AI email generator](https://www.chaserhq.com/features/ai-email-generator).

Important bookkeeping correction: [Aider's Karbon integration](https://karbonhq.com/en-GB/integrations/aider/) already checks uploaded versus requested documents and follows up on mismatches. A standalone overlay might suit firms not using those tools, but that is unvalidated positioning, not a missing market feature.

Agency delivery/scope briefs face low-price direct competition too: [ScopeFence](https://www.scopefence.com/) advertises email-based scope assistance and a $14.99 monthly Pro plan; [AgencyAnalytics](https://agencyanalytics.com/pricing) already includes AI reporting. Rank this last without an audience or unusual domain advantage.

## Ideas deprioritized after research

- Generic security questionnaires: [Conveyor](https://www.conveyor.com/pricing) starts at $9,600/year, but [ResponseHub](https://responsehub.ai/pricing/) already offers a $149/month entry tier. Competing solely on lower price or citations is insufficient.
- Generic contractor follow-up: [Jobber](https://help.getjobber.com/en/articles/automations/) includes quote and invoice follow-ups; [Hatch](https://www.usehatchapp.com/solutions/estimate-follow-up) offers AI estimate follow-up.
- Construction voice-to-change-orders: [Clearstory](https://www.clearstory.build/construction-blog/introducing-clearstory-ai) already offers agents in this workflow.
- Generic Shopify support: [Gorgias](https://www.gorgias.com/blog/ai-agent-pricing) has established per-resolution AI pricing.

## Validation plan

Select two niches and speak with 8–10 buyers in each. Ask to see their most recent actual workflow, frequency, current tools, error/correction burden and purchasing authority. Deliver three sample outputs using approved data. Seek three paid pilots for the same narrowly scoped workflow before building broadly. Suggested test threshold: materially lower total work including review (target 50%), no silent critical omissions in the evaluated cases, repeat usage, and at least two pilot renewals. These are proposed gates, not market benchmarks.

For a 20-customer business, 1 support hour per customer per week consumes 20 hours weekly. Productize onboarding, cap document usage, and measure founder review time from the first pilot. A $10K MRR business is only a sustainable side hustle if this burden stays low.
