# Zusal subscription payments — shortlist for founder choice

Research date: 17 October 2026. Confirmed operating setup: India-based sole proprietorship; possible future Dubai/UAE move. Worldwide English-first developer SaaS; tentative USD 5/month; INR 10,000/month operating budget. No provider selected, account created, quote obtained, or merchant approval received.

## Recommended ranking

1. **Paddle** — recommended first application candidate for worldwide subscription sales. Merchant of record combines subscription checkout, customer portal, payment-related support and customer sales-tax handling. Sole traders are accepted for review; India and UAE not excluded by supplier country list. Advertised 5% + USD 0.50 illustrates USD 0.75 on a USD 5 charge, not an approved quote. Under-USD-10 bespoke pricing can be requested. Monthly payouts/minimum threshold affect early cash flow. Confirm actual India payout fees and terms.
2. **Dodo Payments** — viable alternative MoR with sole-proprietor onboarding and developer-oriented subscriptions. Compare all subscription/international/payout charges, not headline card rate. Agent research flags changed INR payout rules and fixed payout costs; verify current written payout terms before selecting. Future account/entity migration is not guaranteed.
3. **Razorpay Subscriptions** — viable India-based gateway option with international recurring cards subject to activation/approval. May have lower transaction charges, but gateway + subscription fees must be quoted together; do not use domestic headline pricing for global subscriptions. Zusal retains merchant customer-tax/invoicing duties. More operating administration than an MoR for worldwide launch.

MoR handling of customer transaction taxes does not remove Zusal's Indian accounting, income-tax, banking or applicable export/GST obligations. No tax conclusions or rate assumptions are made here.

## Trial implementation recommendation

Zusal grants 30/14-day trial entitlements from the approved activation event. No card is collected. User explicitly initiates a paid subscription checkout when ready; payment-provider webhooks authorize paid access. Keep trial and paid subscription identifiers distinct. This avoids depending on provider-specific cardless-trial limitations and matches the agreed explicit-payment policy. This remains a technical recommendation, not a changed commercial offer.

## Possible Dubai move

Do not optimize launch around speculative relocation. Separate personal relocation from transfer of the seller business. Obtain written provider confirmation of India proprietorship to UAE entity changes, KYC, contracts, payout accounts, subscription/customer portability and any customer action required before undertaking a move. No automatic transfer has been established for any candidate.

## Choice pending and sources

**Decision:** founder selected Paddle. Treat the ranking below as supporting research, not a continuing provider-selection question. Actual merchant approval, applicable small-ticket fees and payout terms remain unverified. No signup, application or production billing setup was performed.

Founder chooses a preferred provider; live use remains conditional on eligibility, approval and acceptable terms. Keep account entitlements independent of provider-specific IDs so integration can be adapted without claiming payment mandates are portable.
