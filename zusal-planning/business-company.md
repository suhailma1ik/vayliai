# Zusal: Indian company options

Research date: 17 October 2026. Planning only. India is a working assumption; founder jurisdiction and citizenship have not been confirmed. Budget: ₹10,000/month operating spend; worldwide $5/month SaaS. No incorporation action taken.

## Recommendation

Include an Indian One Person Company (OPC) among the top three choices for a solo founder. It is a more honest corporate comparison than presenting a conventional private limited company as a one-person structure. Prefer OPC to a conventional private company if the founder wants an incorporated entity now, is eligible, and does not expect an immediate equity cofounder or investor. Prefer a conventional private limited company if actual shared ownership or equity funding is imminent. Either adds company compliance and may be premature before validating paid demand on this budget.

| Consideration | OPC | Conventional private limited company |
|---|---|---|
| Ownership | One member; succession nominee required | At least two members |
| Directors | At least one | At least two |
| Resident director | Companies Act section 149 requires at least one director meeting its India-stay test; OPC member eligibility is a separate rule | Same company resident-director requirement |
| Liability | Company limited by shares can separate company liabilities from shareholder exposure, subject to exceptions | Same general corporate separation |
| Solo fit | Good if eligible and willing to carry company compliance | Requires a real second member/director; do not manufacture a nominal cofounder simply for appearances |
| Equity growth | Must convert before adding another shareholder | Better suited to multiple shareholders, investors and equity compensation |
| Compliance | Accounts, statutory audit, annual financial filings and abridged annual return; reduced meeting requirements do not mean no compliance | Accounts, statutory audit and annual filings; eligible small companies can also use abridged annual returns |
| Payments | Incorporation does not guarantee processor approval, international recurring payments or underwriting acceptance | Same |

## Verified legal foundations

- Companies Act section 3 provides one subscriber for OPC and at least two for a conventional private company; an OPC memorandum must name a nominee with prior written consent. The nominee is a successor member, not necessarily an additional director. [Official government reproduction of section 3](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-3-146).
- Section 149 specifies one director for an OPC and two for a private company, and at least one India-resident director under its 182-day financial-year test (proportional for a newly incorporated company). [Official section 149](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/w/section-149-79).
- The 2021 OPC amendment permits an Indian citizen, resident or otherwise, to incorporate; removes former turnover/capital forced-conversion limits; and allows conversion at any time after the required increases in members/directors. Do not confuse the OPC member residency discussion with section 149's resident-director obligation. [MCA amendment announcement](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=1694828&lang=2&reg=48), [underlying MCA notification](https://www.mca.gov.in/Ministry/pdf/SecondAmndtRules_02022021.pdf).
- Annual company obligations remain. OPC and small-company annual returns use MGT-7A. [MCA annual-return rules](https://www.mca.gov.in/Ministry/pdf/CompaniesMgmtAdminAmndtRules_11032021.pdf). Financial statements and auditor obligations are governed by sections 137 and 139. [Companies Act official consolidated text](https://www.mca.gov.in/content/dam/mca/pdf/CompaniesAct2013.pdf).

## Decision implications

For the product's current stage, limited liability is the main reason to incorporate early; worldwide sales or a professional-looking site alone are not sufficient reasons. A company creates ongoing administrative work before paid validation. Obtain a written first-year and recurring annual compliance quote, including audit, accounting and applicable tax filings, rather than comparing incorporation headline fees. No exact professional fees are estimated here.

Confirm founder nationality/residency, real cofounder plans, customer liability exposure and payment-provider eligibility before final selection. Company choice does not resolve export/GST treatment or foreign recurring-payment approval; those are separate checks. A chartered accountant/company secretary should verify applicability to the founder before incorporation.
