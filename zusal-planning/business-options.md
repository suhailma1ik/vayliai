# Zusal — business setup options for founder selection

Research date: 17 October 2026. India-based solo founder is a working assumption, not a confirmed fact. INR 10,000/month operating budget; tentative USD 5/month global subscription. No business setup selected or registration undertaken.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **Indian sole proprietorship:** recommended for lean paid-demand validation if personal liability is acceptable and no immediate cofounder equity/investment is planned. Lower formation/administration burden than a company, but no separate legal personality or corporate liability separation. This is not the same as saying an unregistered individual automatically qualifies for international payments.
2. **Indian One Person Company (OPC):** recommended if the eligible solo founder wants a company and liability separation from day one. One member, nominee requirement, at least one director; company accounts/audit/filings remain. Obtain an actual professional compliance quote before committing. Convert before adding shareholders.
3. **Conventional Indian private limited company:** strongest fit if real shared ownership or equity funding is imminent. At least two members and two directors; adds governance/compliance before validation. Not recommended merely for a professional appearance or assumed payment access.

OPC and private-company limited liability have legal exceptions; do not describe as complete immunity. Eligibility, registrations and tax/export treatment require the actual founder's facts. No precise accounting fees or tax conclusions are assumed.

## Payment implication

Business structure and payment provider are separate decisions. Stripe India is invite-only; incorporation does not guarantee acceptance. Paddle documents individual/sole-trader onboarding and does not list India among excluded supplier locations, subject to product/domain/identity approval. A merchant of record can handle customer checkout and customer-side sales-tax responsibilities but does not remove the founder's domestic tax/accounting obligations.

At tentative USD 5/month, obtain the applicable small-transaction fee schedule rather than using headline fees uncritically. Do not incorporate abroad merely to work around an assumed payment constraint; no foreign incorporation cost/benefit case has been established.
