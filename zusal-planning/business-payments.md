# Zusal: payment implications for business structure

Research date: 17 October 2026. India-based solo founder is an assumption; worldwide English-first audience, tentative $5/month subscription, ₹10,000/month operating budget are confirmed planning inputs. Research only; no accounts or commitments created.

## Recommendation

Payment access alone does not justify incorporating a company. Start with an Indian sole-proprietor route if legally appropriate for the founder's actual circumstances and if an approved merchant of record (MoR) accepts Zusal. Consider OPC when liability separation warrants ongoing company administration; consider private limited when real co-ownership or fundraising is planned. These are structure recommendations, not guarantees of eligibility. Structural legal details are covered by the companion company/individual research.

Do not register an overseas company merely to bypass Stripe India's onboarding restriction within this budget. Incorporating an Indian company also does not guarantee Stripe access.

## Verified primary-source findings

- Paddle supports individual/sole-trader sellers: its business-identification step explicitly does not apply to them. Domain review and identity verification still apply. India is not on its excluded seller-country list. That establishes potential eligibility, not approval of Zusal or guaranteed payout execution. Sources: [business identification](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification/what-is-business-verification), [account verification](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/account-verification), [seller countries](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/which-countries-are-supported-by-paddle).
- Paddle's advertised pay-as-you-go price is 5% + $0.50, with no monthly charge, but its pricing page specifically asks sellers below $10 to seek bespoke pricing. Applying the advertised formula to $5 gives $0.75, or 15%, before any tax treatment; this is an illustration, not Zusal's confirmed quote. [Pricing](https://www.paddle.com/pricing).
- An MoR manages transaction sales-tax collection/remittance for covered sales. This does not remove the founder's Indian income-tax, accounting, or other applicable obligations. Paddle's transaction role is documented in [supported sales countries](https://developer.paddle.com/concepts/sell/supported-countries-locales/).
- Lemon Squeezy lists India as supported, but bank payouts require Stripe preapproval; without it the published alternative is PayPal. Store approval requires business information and identity verification, and can be refused. [Supported countries](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/supported-countries), [activation](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/activate-your-store).
- Lemon Squeezy's published base is 5% + $0.50, with another 0.5% for subscriptions and potentially 1.5% for international transactions and 1.5% for PayPal customer transactions. Non-US PayPal payouts are separately 3%, capped at $30 per payout. Do not confuse a customer paying with PayPal with merchant payout fees. It also offers low-value custom pricing discussions. [Fees](https://docs.lemonsqueezy.com/help/getting-started/fees).
- Lemon Squeezy's official 2026 update describes investment in Stripe Managed Payments and a planned migration path. Check current onboarding direction and product fit before choosing it as the launch dependency; do not infer Indian Managed Payments eligibility. [Official update](https://www.lemonsqueezy.com/blog/2026-update).
- Stripe India remains invite-only for new accounts and supports a selected number of businesses; an incorporation does not remove this country-level restriction. [Official restriction](https://support.stripe.com/questions/stripe-accounts-are-invite-only-in-india).
- Razorpay officially advertises international-card subscriptions and nearly 100 currencies. Therefore it is wrong to claim it cannot serve international recurring SaaS. Actual merchant international enablement and account configuration require verification; unlike an MoR, do not assume it takes over global sales-tax responsibilities. [Subscriptions](https://razorpay.com/subscriptions/), [international payments](https://razorpay.com/international/).
- An LLP is not a solo-owner workaround: MCA's incorporation guidance requires minimum two designated partners. It is an option only if a real second partner is wanted. [MCA Form 2 instruction kit](https://www.mca.gov.in/LLP/dca/help/instructionkit/1122-Form2LLP_help.PDF).

## Practical consequence for the top three structure choices

| Structure | Payment impact | Fit judgment |
|---|---|---|
| Sole proprietor | Possible Paddle route without company registration; product/domain/identity approval still needed | Best validation-stage default under tight budget if personal-liability tradeoff accepted |
| OPC | Separate company may suit risk/contract needs; no automatic improvement in gateway approval | Best when solo founder already needs a company, subject to ongoing compliance cost |
| Private limited | Suitable when ownership/funding demands it; still subject to provider KYC and country restrictions | Best for a genuine investment/co-founder plan, premature solely for $5 checkout |

The immediate payment validation should be an approved seller/product and verified Indian payout route, followed by an agreed under-$10 fee schedule. None has been obtained. ₹10,000 is operating budget, not confirmed incorporation budget. A company should not be selected based on invented formation or annual-accounting estimates.
